Technical Analysis: Macro Financial Entity Vectors vs. Contradictory Yield & Auction Metrics

Dense vector embeddings (e.g. BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) exhibit high cosine similarity (~0.88-0.92) across financial market reports because articles share macro terminology ("Treasury auction", "FOMC minutes", "S&P 500", "yields", "Reserve Bank of India"). However, opposing numerical directions (e.g. 30-year yield at 5.719% vs 4.85%, RBI 25 bps hike vs 25 bps cut, $39B vs $25B auction size) create severe financial contradiction blindness.

// Formula: Formal Conflict Detection Condition
Conflict_State = ( Cosine_Similarity(Eorig, Ecand) > τsim ) && ( ΔFcontradiction == 1.0 )
where τsim = 0.60, and ΔFcontradiction = 1.0 indicates a numerical yield direction or monetary policy contradiction.
1. Dense Embedding Macro Blindness Dense vectors map "rate hike 25 bps" and "rate cut 25 bps" into close proximity due to identical policy context, missing directional disputes.
2. LLM Evaluation Override Explicit quantitative rule-matching overrides semantic similarity when hard macro disputes (yield deltas, auction figures, central bank actions) occur.
