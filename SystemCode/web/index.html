Sample mode. Fictional demo and test records are included and marked Sample. They are never mixed into the default view.

Events

Each event groups reports about the same development. Event summaries are AI-generated from the linked sources; open an event to see the quotes each statement is based on.

All articles

Every stored news article and SEC filing, newest first, as published by its source. News arrives from the publisher through Alpaca (the data provider); filings come from SEC EDGAR.

Source explorer

Fetch recent records straight from the Alpaca and SEC EDGAR APIs with their data dictionaries. These are live requests made only when you press a button; they are not the stored database and nothing here is saved.

Source labels say who published a record; they are not a judgement of its accuracy. SEC filings are company disclosures, not statements verified by the SEC. AI-generated summaries cite their sources: check the originals before relying on them.
